An independently saved Fuji file matching an exported file's SHA-256 establishes that those two copies match; it does not establish which cache rendition was selected or that the original cached extent was complete. For actual FLAC, an optional full `flac --test` validates decoding and compares the decoded audio to a nonzero STREAMINFO MD5 when present. A zero MD5 or unknown sample count cannot supply that check, and even a successful audio test does not prove the complete original file's byte identity or selection. See [Xiph's FLAC tool documentation](https://xiph.org/flac/documentation_tools_flac.html) and [decoder reference](https://xiph.org/flac/api/group__flac__stream__decoder.html).
